About Sourced Report

Sourced Report is an independent research desk with one job. Build pieces on contested, confusing, or under-covered topics where every claim traces back to a named, dated source. Say plainly where the evidence runs out.

How a piece gets made

Every investigation starts by fanning research out across primary sources: federal data, peer-reviewed literature, court and legislative records, independent investigative journalism. Not one narrative followed downhill. Findings get cross-checked against each other before anything gets written.

Before publication, each piece goes through an adversarial fact-checking pass built to survive scrutiny from people who know the subject better than we do. That means hunting for overstated causal claims, statistics used outside their original context, conflicts of interest in the underlying research, and legal or scientific claims that need more precise language than a first draft usually has.

Sourced Report's research and drafting process is AI-assisted. That's how one person can fan out across dozens of primary sources and hold a piece to this standard without a newsroom behind them. A human editor reviews and directs every draft before it publishes. Every piece discloses that process instead of hiding it.

The standard

  • Named sources, not vibes. Every factual claim is traceable to a specific, dated, citable source.
  • Conflicts of interest get flagged. Including in the studies and reports a piece relies on, not just in Sourced Report's own reporting.
  • Uncertainty is labeled, not smoothed over. If the evidence is thin, contested, or doesn't exist yet, the piece says so.
  • Every piece ships with its own sourcing note. What was checked, what got left out and why, where the limits of the evidence are.

Where this is headed

This is issue one. Sourced Report is currently a one-person operation, built to prove the model works before it scales. More investigations are in progress.
